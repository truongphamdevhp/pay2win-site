How do I play?

Hold your finger on the screen and drag the character to dodge everything flying in. The longer you survive, the higher your score and the more Prestige you earn. Tap the X at the top left to leave a run and open the other tabs.

How do I restore my Growth Tokens?

Open the Store tab and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy them. Restoring is free.

I bought a Growth Token but my size didn't change

  1. Open the Store tab and tap “Restore purchases”.
  2. Check that you're signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.
  3. If you used Ask to Buy, the token arrives after the purchase is approved.

If it still doesn't show up, contact us with the date of the purchase. Don't send payment details.

I deleted the app. Is my progress gone?

Growth Tokens come back with “Restore purchases”. Progress earned by playing (Prestige from runs, best run, top runs) is stored only on the device, so it's lost when the app is deleted.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is approved, that Growth Token and its 100 Prestige are removed from the game.

Is the leaderboard based on money?

No. Ranks use virtual Prestige Points only. Growth Tokens have no cash value and there are no prizes.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
