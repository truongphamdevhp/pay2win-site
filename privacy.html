The short version

Pay2Win collects no personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

  • No account
  • No analytics
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No third-party SDKs

Data stored on your device

To keep your progress between sessions, the app saves these items locally on your iPhone:

  • Your display name (a default guest name)
  • Prestige earned by playing
  • Your best run, lifetime dodges, and your top 10 runs (score and time)
  • How many Growth Tokens you own, kept only as a cache for quick startup

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. Growth Tokens can be restored afterwards because Apple keeps your purchase history, but progress earned by playing can't be recovered.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, email, payment details, or Apple ID. The app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device which Growth Tokens you own. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
